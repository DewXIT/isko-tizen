# Community code of conduct

People with different experience levels and backgrounds are welcome here. These
expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas without personal attacks.
- Make room for questions and different levels of technical knowledge.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, spam or publish another person's private data.
- Ask before sharing someone else's recordings, logs or identifying information.
- Keep discussions relevant and respect reasonable maintainer moderation requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads and limit
participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and repeated harm.
For abusive content, use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls;
do not repost private information in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities have
a separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).

This is a volunteer project. Be patient with reviews and avoid demanding response
deadlines from contributors or maintainers.
